# amuzesh Audit Report — 2026-11-09

**Scope:** a P(-1) hardening, optimization and repair sweep (first-party standards,
*P(-1): Hardening*, and the *Security Hardening* checklist) over the whole repository
outside vendored `lib/`: `src/`, `tests/`, `scripts/`, CI and release workflows, and the
documents. **Baseline:** 0.2.1 (commit `8abde51`, cyrius 6.7.18, ganita 1.2.15,
tyche 1.2.2). **Result:** 0.2.2.

## Executive summary

amuzesh validated nothing. Every public function trusted its shapes, counts and labels,
and the matrix accessors underneath (`ganita_mat_get` / `_set`) are unchecked by design.
That gave one **HIGH** finding: a class or cluster label taken from the caller's data was
used as a row index for a heap write. A label of 2^40 killed the process. A label equal to
`ncls` silently overwrote the neighbouring prototype. Degenerate sizes crashed (`n = 0`
SIGFPE; `k = 0` and a huge `max_iter` SIGSEGV), and one NaN in the data gave a model
with 33% purity and no signal.

0.2.2 validates every public entry point and fails in ganita's vocabulary (0, −1 / −2,
NaN; [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-failure-vocabulary-and-validation.md)). It also fixes four
correctness defects, each reproduced first:

- **Empty classes were predicted.** A class with no training rows kept a zero prototype,
  and a query near the origin was classified as that class.
- **A fit cut short by `max_iter` described the wrong centroids**, and nothing in the
  model said whether it had converged.
- **The mean of identical rows was not exact**, so k-means on duplicated data marched its
  labels for up to k extra passes and broke its own inertia invariant. The new fuzz
  harness found this.
- **Every Lloyd pass leaked its scratch** into the never-freeing heap.

The kernels are 2.2–4.3× faster with bit-identical decisions, and the test, bench and
fuzz suites are new. Nothing found reaches beyond the process: amuzesh has no I/O,
parsing, syscalls or process surface.

## Method

- **Toolchain sweeps (6.7.18)**, before and after: `cyrius audit` (fmt / lint / docs /
  tests / bench), `cyrius coverage`, `cyrius doc --check`, `cyrius deny`,
  `cyrius vet src/main.cyr`, `cyrius lint --strict-deferrals`, `cyrius deps --verify`.
  Before: fmt, deny and vet were clean; 9 lint warnings; 18 undocumented public
  functions; coverage 15/21 (71%). After: all clean, 0 undocumented, coverage 21/23
  (91%; the two unreferenced functions are the demo's own).
- **Manual review** of every non-vendored file, and a diff of the scaffold (workflows,
  `.gitattributes`, test templates) against what `cyrius init` 6.7.18 writes.
- **Reproductions before any fix.** Ten programs against the 0.2.1 source:
  - five crashes: label 2^40 in `amz_proto_fit` and a −2^42 class in `amz_purity`
    (SIGSEGV); fit with `n = 0` (SIGFPE); fit with `k = 0` and with `max_iter = 2^61`
    (SIGSEGV);
  - five silent failures: a label equal to `ncls` (prototype 0 read 0, should be 3); one
    NaN in X (inertia NaN, purity 33%); 592 B leaked per `amz_kmeans_update`; a
    `max_iter = 1` model (inertia 528 against 207 for its own centroids); an empty
    class predicted at x = 1.
  Each is now a regression assertion in `tests/amuzesh.tcyr`.
- **Bit identity.** A probe built against both trees compared the blobs, the k-means++
  seeds, the assignment inertia, and the fit's labels and inertia: all identical (see
  *Result impact*).
- **Numerical accuracy.** The fitted centroids were compared against exact rational means
  (Python `fractions`) for three mean formulas: 0.2.1's `sum·(1/n)`, `sum / n`, and
  Welford's running mean.
- **Fuzzing.** `tests/amuzesh.fcyr` (new): 10,000 rounds of randomized shapes and hostile
  arguments against every public function, with the contract decided independently in
  the harness.
- **Mutation testing.** 14 hand-written mutants, one per repaired defect or
  load-bearing check. All 14 are killed; 11 by both the unit suite and the fuzz harness.
- **Cross-architecture.** aarch64 under qemu-user: the unit suite (which pins exact bit
  patterns), the fuzz harness and the demo.
- **Benchmarks.** `scripts/bench-history.sh` (new) before and after, in one session,
  median of three runs.

## Findings

### HIGH — fixed

| # | Finding | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **An out-of-range label was a heap write at a caller-chosen offset** (CWE-787). Class labels `y` in `amz_proto_fit` and `amz_purity`, and cluster labels in `amz_kmeans_update`, indexed `ganita_mat_set` / `store64` with no range check. The written value was a running sum of the caller's own data. A label of 2^40 crashed. A label equal to `ncls` wrote into the next allocation and corrupted the result silently: prototype 0 came back 0 instead of 3. Training labels are external data. | `src/proto.cyr`, `src/kmeans.cyr` | Every label is checked against `[0, k)` / `[0, ncls)` before anything is written. Refusal is 0, `AMZ_ERR_ARG` or NaN. Seven assertions and the fuzz harness cover it (a label pushed to `k`, −1 or 2^40 in one round in 16). |

### MEDIUM — fixed

| # | Finding | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Degenerate sizes crashed.** `n = 0` divided by zero in the first k-means++ draw (SIGFPE). `k = 0` made `ganita_mat_new` return 0, which was then written through (CWE-476). `k > n` silently produced duplicate centers. | `src/kmeans.cyr` | Shapes and `1 ≤ k ≤ n` are validated; every `ganita_mat_new` and `alloc` result is checked. |
| 3 | **`max_iter` was unbounded.** `max_iter * 8` wrapped or passed `ALLOC_MAX`, `alloc` returned 0, and the history was written through null (SIGSEGV at 2^61). Below that, any budget allocated its full history, never freed. | `amz_kmeans_fit` | `1 ≤ max_iter ≤ AMZ_KMEANS_MAX_ITER` (10,000, a policy cap; ADR 0002). Every allocation goes through `_amz_alloc_words`, which refuses a count that would wrap. |
| 4 | **NaN and infinity were accepted silently.** One NaN in X: NaN inertia, purity 33%, no signal. A NaN distance never wins `f64_lt`, so argmins defaulted to index 0. | every judging function | Refused by ganita ADR 0004's rule. A judging function (fit, seeding, assign, prototype fit, predict, accuracy) refuses a non-finite entry, tested on the exponent bits. `amz_kmeans_update` carries and propagates. |
| 5 | **A class with no training rows was predicted.** Its prototype stayed at zero ("never selected on real data"), and real features are often centered. With classes at {5, 7} and {−7, −5}, x = 1 went to the empty class. | `src/proto.cyr` | The empty class's row is NaN and prediction skips NaN distances, including when class 0 is the empty one ([ADR 0004](../adr/0004-empty-class-has-no-prototype.md)). |
| 6 | **The mean of identical rows was not exact** (found by the new fuzz harness). n copies of x, summed and scaled by 1/n, are generally an ulp off x: seven rows of 0.1 give 0.09999999999999999. The centroid moved off its points. With ties going to the lowest index, the labels then marched through up to k clusters, one per pass, and the inertia rose from 0 to rounding noise. `amz_inertia_monotone`, and with it the demo's gate, rejected the fit, and a small `max_iter` reported "not converged". | `kmeans_update`, `proto_fit` | Means are Welford's running mean (one shared kernel, `_amz_label_means`). The increment for an identical row is exactly 0, so the mean is that row. It is also more accurate in general: on the test fixture the worst coordinate is 1.43 ulp from the exact mean, against 2.03 for 0.2.1's `sum·(1/n)` (and 2.43 for `sum / n`). Seven identical rows now converge at pass 2 with inertia exactly 0. |
| 7 | **A fit that ran out of `max_iter` returned inconsistent results.** The last pass updated the centroids after assigning, so the returned labels and inertia described the previous centroids. Repro: inertia 528 against 207 for the returned centroids. Nothing told a converged fit from a cut one: `max_iter = 2` and `max_iter = 50` both reported 2 iterations. | `amz_kmeans_fit` | The final update is skipped when the budget is spent, so labels, inertia and centroids always agree. A new model slot and `amz_model_converged()` report the ending, and the demo prints it. |

### Performance — decisions bit-identical

| # | Change | Effect (same session, median of 3) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Direct row access** ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-direct-row-access.md)): one row address per row, then `load64`, instead of two non-inlined `ganita_mat_get` calls per element. A unit test pins the layout against `ganita_mat_get`. | `sqdist` 100 → 38 ns |
| P2 | **Partial-distance search** (Bei & Gray 1985) in assignment, the k-means++ fold and prediction. Adding a non-negative square never lowers an f64 sum, so a candidate cut at `≥ bound` could not have won. | `kmeans_assign` −27%, `kmeans_fit` −28% at d = 8 (−39% at d = 32) |
| P3 | **k-means++** stops its walk at the pick, folds the next total into the minimum-distance pass, and skips the fold after the last center. | `kmeans_pp_seed` 855 → 203 µs |
| P4 | **The fit** allocates once and swaps label buffers instead of copying. | `kmeans_fit` 3.83 → 0.89 ms |
| P5 | **Purity** O(k·n) → O(n + k + ncls). | 12.8 → 11.6 µs at k = 8; 797 → 14 µs at k = 512 |

Full table: [`../benchmarks.md`](../benchmarks.md). Every 0.2.2 row includes the new
validation.

## Observations — not changed, and why

- **A raw label array's length cannot be checked.** It carries none. amuzesh reads the
  `n` entries the caller states, and `SECURITY.md` and `docs/api.md` say so.
- **`amz_sqdist`, `amz_copy_row` and the `_amz_*` kernels stay unchecked**, like
  `ganita_mat_get`. A per-element branch in the inner loop is what the entry-point
  validation exists to avoid.
- **The standalone kernels allocate per call** (`amz_kmeans_pp_seed`,
  `amz_kmeans_update`, `amz_purity`). A scratch-taking variant would let a caller loop
  without growing the heap. Deferred until a consumer needs one.
- **Squared distances overflow to +∞ for coordinate gaps above about 1.3e154.** Ties
  then go to the lowest index. Finite input is accepted because it has a defined, if
  saturated, answer. The running mean removes the sum's own overflow at n·max|x|.
- **An empty k-means cluster keeps its previous centroid.** Some libraries relocate it
  to a far point instead. Retention is the documented 0.1.0 choice and is
  deterministic.
- **Fitting reseeds tyche's single global stream**, a side effect on the caller's stream,
  and nothing is thread-safe. A per-model RNG needs a tyche state API that its frozen 1.x
  surface does not have.
- **The first k-means++ draw is `u64 % n`.** Its modulo bias is at most n / 2^63.
- **ganita could export a row accessor**, which would retire ADR 0003's layout
  dependency. That is an upstream suggestion, not a defect.
- **CI's actions stay at the `cyrius init` template's SHAs** (checkout v4, gh-release v2).
  tyche moved to newer releases on 2026-11-04. Upgrading actions is its own change.
- **The installer is fetched from cyrius `main`** (`curl … | sh`), the template's form.
  tyche's audit gives the trade-off.
- **`CLAUDE.md`'s identity and goal lines are still TODO.** Those are product decisions
  for the maintainer, not audit repairs.

## Result impact of 0.2.2

- **Bit-identical to 0.2.1:** the blobs, the k-means++ seeds, every assignment and its
  inertia, and on the test fixture the fit's labels and inertia. The demo's output is
  byte-identical to 0.2.1's, on x86_64 and on aarch64.
- **Moved by 1–2 ulp:** fitted centroids and prototypes (the running mean, finding 6),
  toward the exact mean on the fixture.
- **Changed behavior:** every input 0.2.1 crashed on or silently mis-answered is now
  refused. An empty class's prototype is NaN and is never predicted. A budget-exhausted
  fit returns the centroids its labels describe, with `converged = 0`. Duplicate-heavy
  data converges in two passes instead of up to k + 1.
